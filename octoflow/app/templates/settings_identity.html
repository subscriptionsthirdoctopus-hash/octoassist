How this works

Each identity provider lets your users sign in with their organisation's Microsoft account. When somebody signs in through an IdP for the first time, OctoFlow auto-creates their user record in this tenant only. They never see any data from other tenants.

To wire up a new IdP you need three things from your Azure Portal app registration: Tenant ID, Application (client) ID, and a fresh Client secret Value. Add a redirect URI shown below to the app registration.
