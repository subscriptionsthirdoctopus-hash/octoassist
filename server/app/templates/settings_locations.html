How this works

When a new ticket is created, OctoAssist stamps it with a location derived from the reporter's primary laptop (or their Entra office). If that location matches one of the rules below, the ticket is automatically assigned to the named technician at create-time. Otherwise the ticket stays unassigned and shows up in the default queue.
