How this works

CAB (Change Advisory Board) members are the people who review and approve production changes. Once a user is in the CAB:

  • They get emailed on every new change submitted (review + approve at /changes).
  • They get FYI emails on every new incident / service request created — so leadership sees what's hitting the helpdesk.

Users in this list come from your Entra ID directory sync (or were created locally). Toggle the checkbox to add or remove.
