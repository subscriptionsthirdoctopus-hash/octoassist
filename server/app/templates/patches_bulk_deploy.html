How this works

Tick the endpoints you want patched in the left table, tick the specific packages in the right table, then click Deploy. OctoAssist creates a Patch Window with hand-picked targets and starts it immediately (or at the scheduled time). Agents pick the job up on their next 30-second poll and install via PSWindowsUpdate / winget as SYSTEM.
