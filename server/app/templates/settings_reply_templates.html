How this works

Reusable text snippets a technician can drop into the reply box on any ticket. Keeps language consistent across the team and saves typing on the common cases — acknowledge, ask for details, escalate, mark as waiting on user, resolved-please-verify, etc. Pick from the dropdown on /tickets detail page.

Tip: use placeholders like {name}, {ticket}, {category} — the staff page will swap them with real values from the ticket before populating the textarea.
