📋 How auditors use these reports

OctoAssist is the evidence source for several Annex A controls. During an ISO 27001:2022 surveillance or certification audit, the auditor typically asks for: a sample of incidents across a window (A.5.24-A.5.26), the asset inventory snapshot (A.5.9), the access register with role + status (A.5.16), and a complete change log with CAB sign-off (A.8.32). Each report above exports a UTF-8 CSV with UTC timestamps, suitable for direct attachment to your audit file.
