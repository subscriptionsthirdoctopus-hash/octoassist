The state of IT operations

Most mid-market IT teams are running ITSM out of email and spreadsheets.

That's fine until you face an audit, an outage, or a wave of new joiners. The tools that fix it — ServiceNow, BMC Helix, Ivanti — solve enterprise-scale problems with enterprise-scale licences. OctoAssist is built for the gap in the middle: ITIL rigor, audit-ready evidence, and asset visibility, at a price that doesn't require a board paper.

  • Tickets get lost in shared inboxes and WhatsApp groups
  • SLAs are aspirational with no breach detection or audit trail
  • Asset inventory is stale and built on quarterly spreadsheets
  • Patch posture is unknown until something gets exploited
  • Audits become fire-drills because evidence isn't continuous
