What are Asset Groups?

Asset Groups allow you to group custom sets of endpoints together. Once configured:
• Under Software (SAM): Select a product, choose "Asset Group" target mode, select your group, and deploy installations, updates, or silent uninstalls fleet-wide.
• Under Patches (Bulk Deploy): Instantly select all Windows endpoints in your curated group to deploy Windows Updates in one click.
